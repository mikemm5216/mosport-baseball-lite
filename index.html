How Baseball Life Works

One set of Attributes. Contact, Power, Command, Defense and the rest are your baseball skills. PMV and TMV are not player ratings.

Traits contain uncertainty. A revealed tag describes a real pattern, but its hidden strength and future effect can differ between careers. Traits can emerge, decay, become dormant or transform.

Career DNA creates possibility, not destiny. Every seed references one of 50 Hall-of-Fame legend archetypes to calibrate the playable historical tail — including 3,000 hits, 500 home runs, 3,000 strikeouts and Hall of Fame careers.

Programs and prospect paths are real baseball structures. Youth national teams, rankings, drafts and farm systems create different routes through the same career game.

Position changes require baseball authority. Your primary position or pitching role never changes automatically because of ratings. Only a coach, team or organization decision can trigger a move.

Life events remain part of the career. Training, family, media, academics, injuries, surgery, rehab, integrity, relationships, scouts, contracts, trades and national-team events can all change your path.
